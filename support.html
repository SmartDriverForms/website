Start here when you need help

Quick help for account questions and the current Smart Driver Forms app.

If the information below does not solve the problem, contact support and we will help you from there.

Quick account help

Sign-in and account questions

Sign-In Problems

If you cannot sign in, first make sure you are using the email address connected to your Smart Driver Forms account. If the problem continues, contact Support with that email address.

Account Confirmation

If your account confirmation is incomplete or you are unsure whether your account is ready to use, contact Support with the email address used to create the account.

Forgot Your Password?

On the app sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password?, enter your Smart Driver Forms account email, and use the reset link sent to your email.

Delete Your Account

Account deletion is available inside the app. You can also review the account-deletion information on this website.

Contact Support
