For fleets

Fleet tools are planned, but not available yet

Smart Driver Forms is being built driver-first, with fleet tools planned around the same trip and document workflows.

The current public product is the driver app. Fleet access, company management, and fleet reporting are future parts of the platform and are not open for customer use yet.

What the fleet side is intended to help organize

These are planned directions, not currently released fleet features. Details may change as the driver-side platform continues to develop.

Planned

Driver Records

Organize company driver information and give fleets a clearer view of the records connected to their drivers.

Planned

Trip Sheets

Provide a company-side way to review finalized driver trip sheets and related trip information.

Planned

Documents

Bring driver and trip documents into a more organized company workflow as document features are developed.

Planned

Reports

Give fleets useful summaries built from verified driver and trip data instead of requiring the same information to be rebuilt by hand.

Planned

Company Organization

Create one place for future company-level driver, trip, document, and account organization.

Built from the driver side out

Driver data first. Fleet tools second.

The fleet side is intended to use information already created through driver workflows where that makes sense, instead of making drivers and office staff enter the same information again.
